Delete your account and data

To delete your Purple account and the personal data associated with it:

  • In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • Or by email: write to privacy@purplechaamba.com from your registered number's email, or include your phone number, and we'll process it.

We delete your personal data (profile, cards, photos, messages) within 30 days of the request, except limited records we must keep for legal or anti-abuse reasons.

Common questions

I didn't get my OTP. Wait a minute and retry; check your number and signal. Still stuck — email support.

How do I report someone? Use the report option on a card or message, or email support with details.
