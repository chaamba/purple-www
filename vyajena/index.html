Vyajena

A quiet, end-to-end encrypted chat for one private group. Its admin runs the server and invites each member.

Getting in

You need an invite from your group's admin. Paste it in the app and tap Join. Until the admin's device adds you, the app says it is waiting.

Remove your account

Ask your group's admin to remove you, or write to privacy@purplechaamba.com. Removing you deletes your username, your devices and their keys, and anything still waiting for you on the server. See the privacy policy.
