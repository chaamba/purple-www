Letters, not feeds.

Purple is a slower, kinder way to meet people near you — written cards, sent with intention, instead of an endless scroll.

How it works

  • Write a postcard, polaroid, or calling card and send it to people in your area.
  • Receive letters in your inbox and reply when something resonates.
  • No public feed, no like counts — just correspondence.

Get the app

Purple is currently in invite-only testing on Android. If a friend invited you, check your email for the install link.
